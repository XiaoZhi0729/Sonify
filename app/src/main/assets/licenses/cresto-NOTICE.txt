SonifyDialog.kt adapts the centered dialog geometry, button layout and enter/exit
motion from app/ui/components/glasense/GlasenseDialog.kt and glasense-ui tokens.
DialogMaterial.kt adapts the thin material recipe and AGSL shader from
app/ui/components/glasense/material/{MaterialRecipes,MaterialRecipeRenderEffect,
MaterialShaderCode}.kt.

Modified for Sonify: application-local overlay registration, Backdrop 1.0.5,
slot-based content, asynchronous enabled/error states, responsive width,
keyboard insets, accessibility, local theme and compatibility fallback.
The complete glasense-ui module is not included. CrestoDialogStyle.kt includes
its Blue500/Red500 tokens; their Tailwind MIT notice is retained in
cresto-THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. CrestoDialogEffects.kt adapts GlasenseHighlight,
GlasenseDialog shadow drawing and glasense-ui DimIndication.
CoverArtworkEffect.kt adapts the about-page card interactions from
app/ui/modifier/TiltModifier.kt (tiltOnPress) and app/ui/modifier/RippleModifier.kt
(shaderRipple / RIPPLE_SHADER): 3D tilt-on-press plus the expanding ring ripple and
the outline highlight, with the same angles, springs, timing, ring geometry and
opacity curve.

Modified for Sonify: the press state is driven by the tap node's InteractionSource
(no hand-rolled pointerInput, so the shell's vertical collapse drag and the
accessibility semantics are untouched), the ripple is drawn as a Compose radial
gradient instead of the AGSL runtime shader (no API 33 requirement), the tilt is
taken from the press point only, and all animation values are read in the
layer/draw phase. The chromatic press-indent shader (SuperGraphic.kt) is not ported.
